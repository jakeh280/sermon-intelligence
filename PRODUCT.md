# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Church media directors and creative teams use Sermon Intelligence after a service to turn a sermon transcript into publishable YouTube metadata and social clip ideas. They need to move quickly, understand the workflow immediately, and trust the output enough to refine and publish it.

## Product Purpose

Sermon Intelligence is a free online utility that converts sermon transcripts into practical distribution assets. Success means a first-time visitor can supply a transcript, set their clip range, generate useful output, and copy it into their publishing workflow without friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, creatively grounded. The experience should feel like an experienced studio tool from Overflow Creative: polished and encouraging, never corporate, flashy, or mysterious.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-dashboard decoration, crowded SaaS controls, tiny hard-to-read labels, and an overbuilt premium-product feel that conflicts with the tool being free and approachable.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable.
2. Treat generated content as useful creative material, not technical output.
3. Use restraint so the blue accent marks decisions and progress.
4. Make reading comfortable for long transcript and output sessions.
5. Keep the tool welcoming to a first-time church media volunteer and efficient for a practiced director.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard expectations. Default reading text should be at least 1rem, with clear focus states, readable line lengths, and motion that does not obstruct the workflow.
